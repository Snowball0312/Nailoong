# Reference Analysis — Nailong Three-View Sheet

## Intake decision

One composite orthographic sheet shows the same stylized, organic character from front, side,
and rear. It is suitable for a stylized procedural reconstruction and projection checks. The
source is a rendered reference sheet rather than calibrated orthographic photography, so the
measurements below are approximate and the exact hidden surface is not recoverable.

## Measured observations

Image canvas: 1004 × 769 px. Approximate character bounds:

| View | Approximate bounds (px) | Height | Maximum width |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Front | x=36–378, y=122–691 | 569 px | 342 px |
| Side | x=398–622, y=122–691 | 569 px | 224 px |
| Back | x=633–974, y=122–691 | 569 px | 341 px |

The characters are near the same height. The front and back are bilaterally symmetric. The
front silhouette has a rounded, tapered crown and narrow neck/shoulder transition, broad
abdomen and hips, then two separated legs that taper toward the ankles. The side silhouette
shows a forward-projecting belly and chest, a shallow rear contour, a small forward muzzle, and
arms hanging close to the torso. The back is smooth and has no visible tail or markings.

## Component hierarchy

- Macro: one continuous head-neck-torso mass; paired arms; paired legs and feet.
- Meso: shoulder-to-arm transitions; wrists and hands; hips, ankles, and flattened feet.
- Micro: two green eye rings with dark pupils; tiny closed mouth; cream abdominal marking;
  dark, simple hand/finger ends and matching foot/toe ends.

## Relative placement and surface

- The head has no separate neck seam; the head narrows into a long, slim neck that widens
  gradually into the shoulders and torso.
- Arms emerge from the upper lateral torso with a continuous organic transition, hang at the
  sides, and end around the lower abdomen/hip level.
- Legs emerge beneath the broad lower torso, diverge, and taper to broad flattened feet.
- The cream belly marking is a flush/inset surface region that follows the torso curvature; it is
  not a protruding ellipsoid.
- Warm yellow/orange body surface is matte-to-satin with soft lighting gradients. Hands and feet
  have matching dark neutral end color. Eyes are small relative to the head, with green irises and
  dark pupils, not spherical protrusions.
- The mouth is a short, subtle closed smile with a soft lip edge.

## Proportion estimates (front view)

Using visible figure height as 1.0:

- Head including narrow neck transition: approximately 0.29 of total height.
- Maximum torso width: approximately 0.43 of total height.
- Shoulder width: approximately 0.39 of total height.
- Hip width: approximately 0.39 of total height.
- Hip-to-floor leg length: approximately 0.29 of total height.
- Belly marking: approximately 0.69 of torso maximum width and 0.30 of total height.

These are silhouette measurements, not anatomical canon. Shoulder/hip boundaries are gradual,
so their exact landmark rows are uncertain.

## Unknowns and confidence

- Confidence high: front/back symmetry, outer silhouette, yellow/cream/dark color regions,
  hanging arms, split legs, and smooth unmarked back.
- Confidence medium: exact belly inset depth, limb cross-sections, feet thickness, and facial
  protrusion; these are inferred from the side view and soft render shading.
- Confidence low: rear-side depth profile between the side and back views, undersides of the feet,
  and hidden hand/toe segmentation.
- No tail, dorsal ridge, clothing, or additional markings are visible; none are added.

## Reconstruction constraints

Use a shared lofted torso profile constrained by front width and side depth samples. Shape arms as
overlapping continuous sweeps whose roots begin inside the torso and share its material. Make the
belly region conform to the same torso surface and use a slight inset boundary, not a floating
surface patch. Keep the head/eyes low-relief and the mouth closed and small. Keep hands/feet
consistent in end color; model two toes per foot. Compare renders with the three source panels
using matched front, side, and rear cameras.
